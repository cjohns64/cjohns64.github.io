Space Survivor

This game was made for the University of Oregon Quackhacks 2025 event. I was part of a team of 4 people. We all worked on the programming, but I was also responsible for the 3D models and the skybox. We completed the project from start to finish in just 24 hours, since that was the goal of the hackathon. The game itself is a simple clone of Vampire Survivors, with the added twist of being in full 3D with art direction inspired by Galaga.

Our project received an honorable mention in the game category of the event. The event had a total of 41 projects submitted into about 6 categories.
